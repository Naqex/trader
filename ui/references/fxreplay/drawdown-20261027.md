## Local calculation contract

- True account equity includes floating PnL only when a source explicitly
  declares that basis, scope and applicable filters. Otherwise label the chart
  closed balance; never rename closed-balance drawdown to equity.
- For each continuous segment, keep the running peak. The drawdown magnitude
  is peak minus current value, floored at zero; chart values are negative.
  Percent is magnitude / positive peak × 100. Missing observations break the
  series, and segment changes reset the peak.
- Main chart renders underwater observations rather than padding categories
  with every peak/zero point. A known all-zero series retains a zero observation;
  unknown values remain unknown. Equity dates are observation timestamps;
  balance dates are the report's close/event timestamps, in the selected report
  timezone. This is a deliberate difference from FX's entry-date categories.
- Maximum is the largest observed magnitude. Average is the arithmetic mean
  of known underwater observations, not an inferred FX backend episode average.
  Partial/sampled account observations keep their existing coverage notice.
- A drawdown episode begins on crossing below the previous peak, ending on
  regaining that peak. Recovery is elapsed time from peak to recovery, averaged
  over recovered episodes with valid timestamps. An unfinished episode does
  not supply a made-up recovery duration.
- MAE is the observed adverse price distance / original stop distance. Read
  `excursion.mae_r` only with `r_basis=initial_stop_distance`. Keep known zero;
  never substitute a missing observation or configured simulation stop.
- Histogram counts observed trades only. Its regular intervals are
  `[start,end)`; values at/above the cap enter `≥cap`. FX tests `>cap` despite
  its displayed `≥cap`; local fixes that boundary and floating decimal division.
- Winning MAE values are nonnegative magnitudes. Average, min and max use the
  whole winning group only when every winner has a valid observation and no
  classification is unknown. Otherwise display `N/A` and coverage in help.
  FX's signed negative R values are negated for its cards; the local positive
  magnitude convention gives the same ordering without reversing its extrema.
- Empty FX winner arrays visibly produce `∞/-∞`; local preserves `N/A`.
  Very small real percentages use enough decimals to avoid misleading `-0%`.
